# Pat's Tech Help — Website & App Build Pricing

## E-Commerce Packages

### Starter — $500
**Best for:** Small sellers with a simple product line (handmade goods, a single service, 1–10 products)

- Up to 10 products
- Product listing page + individual product pages
- Stripe checkout (credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- Order confirmation email to customer
- Mobile-friendly design
- Contact page
- Basic branding (colors, logo, font)

---

### Business — $1,000
**Best for:** Growing shops with a real catalog and need for polish (25–75 products, local business going online)

Everything in Starter, plus:
- Up to 75 products
- Product categories / filtering
- Coupon/discount code support
- Customer account / order history
- About page + FAQ page
- Google Analytics setup
- Custom domain setup (if needed)

---

### Premium — $1,500
**Best for:** Serious e-commerce operations — multiple categories, inventory tracking, complex needs

Everything in Business, plus:
- Unlimited products
- Inventory tracking (low stock warnings, out-of-stock handling)
- Shipping rate calculator at checkout
- Admin dashboard to manage products, view orders, mark shipped
- Subscription / recurring billing option (e.g. monthly boxes)
- SEO optimization (meta tags, sitemap, structured data)
- Post-launch support: 30 days of bug fixes included

## Notes

- 50% deposit required to start, 50% on launch
- Hosting is the client's responsibility (~$5–20/month depending on platform)
- Domain registration not included (~$12–15/year, client pays)
- Stripe processing fees (2.9% + 30¢ per transaction) are paid by the client directly to Stripe — not included in build price
- Source code is transferred to client on final payment
